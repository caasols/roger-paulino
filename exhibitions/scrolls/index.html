Scrolls

Buraco, Lisboa

23 May - 4 Jul 2026

Scrolls departs from the two artists' desire to return to primitive modes of representation, combining works of drawing with installative paintings.

Buraco, Calçada do Moinho de Vento 16B, 1150-206 Lisboa. Open Saturdays, 3pm to 7pm. With the support of Câmara Municipal de Lisboa.
